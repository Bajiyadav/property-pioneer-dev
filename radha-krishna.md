# 🌸 श्री राधा कृष्ण (Radha Krishna) 🌸

> **"हरे कृष्ण हरे कृष्ण, कृष्ण कृष्ण हरे हरे ।**  
> **हरे राम हरे राम, राम राम हरे हरे ॥"**
>
> _"Where there is righteousness, there is victory; where there is Krishna and Radha's grace, there is peace, wisdom, and boundless success."_

---

## 🕉️ Sacred Invocations & Mantras

### 1. Maha-Mantra (महामंत्र)

```text
हरे कृष्ण हरे कृष्ण कृष्ण कृष्ण हरे हरे ।
हरे राम हरे राम राम राम हरे हरे ॥
```

_Hare Krishna Hare Krishna, Krishna Krishna Hare Hare_  
_Hare Rama Hare Rama, Rama Rama Hare Hare_

### 2. Sri Radha Krishna Pranam Mantra

```text
तप्तकाञ्चनगौराङ्गि राधे वृन्दावनेश्वरि ।
वृषभानुसुते देवि प्रणमामि हरिप्रिये ॥
```

> _O Goddess Radhika, whose complexion is like molten gold, the Queen of Vrindavan, the precious daughter of King Vrishabhanu, and the eternal beloved of Sri Krishna — I offer my heartfelt prostrations unto You._

```text
हे कृष्ण करुणासिन्धो दीनबन्धो जगत्पते ।
गोपेश गोपिकाकान्त राधाकान्त नमोऽस्तु ते ॥
```

> _O Krishna, boundless ocean of compassion, friend of the humble, Lord of the cosmos, protector of cows, and dearest beloved of the Gopis and Sri Radha — I bow down to You._

## 🌺 Eternal Wisdom & Principles for Life & Action

1. **निष्काम कर्म (Selfless Dedicated Action - Gita 2.47)**

   > _कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन ।_  
   > _मा कर्मफलहेतुर्भूर्मा ते सङ्गोऽस्त्वकर्मणि ॥_  
   > **Meaning:** You have a right to perform your prescribed duty, but you are not entitled to the fruits of action. Never consider yourself the sole cause of the results of your activities, and never be attached to inaction. Put 100% devotion and excellence into every task.

2. **अनन्य भक्ति (Unyielding Focus & Devotion - Gita 9.22)**

   > _अनन्याश्चिन्तयन्तो मां ये जनाः पर्युपासते ।_  
   > _तेषां नित्याभियुक्तानां योगक्षेमं वहाम्यहम् ॥_  
   > **Meaning:** To those who constantly meditate upon Me with single-minded devotion, I carry what they lack, and preserve what they have.

3. **शाश्वत विजय (Truth & Triumph - Gita 18.78)**
   > _यत्र योगेश्वरः कृष्णो यत्र पार्थो धनुर्धरः ।_  
   > _तत्र श्रीर्विजयो भूतिर्ध्रुवा नीतिर्मतिर्मम ॥_  
   > **Meaning:** Wherever there is Krishna, the Lord of all Yoga, and wherever there is Arjuna, the dedicated wielder of the bow, there will surely be fortune, victory, welfare, and steadfast morality.
